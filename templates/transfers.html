🧠 How MedStock Decides a Transfer

1️⃣ Find shortage

Find hospitals where required medicine is greater than current stock.

2️⃣ Find excess

Find other hospitals with stock above their minimum stock level.

3️⃣ Calculate quantity

Recommend the smaller value between available excess and shortage.

4️⃣ Complete transfer

After confirmation, stock is automatically updated in both hospitals.
